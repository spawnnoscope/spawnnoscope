## Hello, I'm spawnnoscope 👋

I build mobile apps — mostly Flutter and Dart, some React Native and TypeScript — and I keep circling the same question: why does one screen feel alive while another feels like a form to fill in? Most of my curiosity goes into motion that follows your finger rather than a clock, and apps that stay useful when the signal drops. I'm still figuring it out in public, one rebuild at a time.
